LLMs in Research 🔬

The Creator of Keras on LLMs in Research​

François Chollet, the creator of Keras and author of "Deep Learning with Python," shared his thoughts on the impact of LLMs in research:

LLMs are having a massive impact on research productivity. Based on my conversations with researchers:

1) Literature review is 5-10x faster
2) Writing is 2-3x faster
3) Coding is 2-3x faster

Overall productivity boost: easily 2-3x.

— François Chollet (@fchollet) December 20, 2023

Nature Survey: Benefits of Generative AI in Research​

A Nature survey of scientists found significant benefits of using generative AI in research. Here's a visualization of the survey results:

Privacy Concerns with LLMs​

When using LLMs, especially commercial models, it's crucial to be aware of privacy implications:

  • LLMs are trained on vast amounts of data, including the inputs you provide.
  • Your prompts, files, and other data sent to commercial LLMs may become part of their training data.

Red Flags 🚩​

Be cautious about sharing:

  • Early drafts of your papers
  • Raw data (especially if covered by an NDA)
  • Personal data (e.g., names, emails, addresses)
caution

Remember that GDPR and other data protection regulations apply when using LLMs. You might inadvertently send data outside the EU.

Solutions for Privacy-Conscious LLM Use​

Despite privacy concerns, there are ways to leverage LLMs safely in research:

  1. ERUDITE at EUR:

    • GPT-4 instance available through MS Teams
    • Safe for sensitive data
    • Currently chat-based only

  2. Host open-source LLMs on Snellius:

    • Use the Dutch supercomputer to run your own LLM
    • Data stays on Snellius, ensuring privacy

  3. oLLAMA for local LLM use:

    • Run small LLMs on your own machine
    • Free, simple, and open-source
    • Enables privacy-secure LLM applications

Nature's Guidelines for LLM Use in Research​

Transparency Requirements​

  • Disclose LLM use in the methods section
  • Specify model version and provider
  • Detail prompt engineering process
  • Explain verification procedures
  • Share prompts in supplementary materials

Authorship Standards​

  • LLMs cannot be authors
  • Authors take responsibility for LLM outputs
  • Content must be fact-checked
  • Cite LLM providers appropriately
  • Document when text is LLM-generated

LLMs in Research Ideation​

Stremersch et al. (2024) discuss generating great research ideas, and GenAI can play a role in this process:

  • LLMs can help generate more ideas, increasing the chances of finding good ones.
  • Use LLMs for supporting tasks like analyzing trends or spaces.
  • Ask LLMs for potential findings for a research idea to challenge your expectations.
  • Fine-tune LLMs on target journal content to align ideas with publication goals.
note

Ideas from LLMs are plentiful, but quality assessment is crucial (Girotra & Netessine, 2023).

Synthetic Respondents and Theorizing​

Recent research explores using LLMs to generate synthetic respondents:

  • Sarstedt et al. (2024) and Argyle et al. (2023) discuss methods for creating synthetic survey respondents.
  • Arora et al. (2025) demonstrate how synthetic respondents can yield qualitative insights for theorizing.

This approach can be valuable for:

  • Pilot testing survey instruments
  • Exploring potential responses to research questions
  • Generating hypotheses for further investigation

LLMs in Coding and Design Choices​

  1. Coding Support: Stay tuned for the second workshop to learn more about using LLMs for coding assistance.

  2. Design Choices: Leverage LLMs to:

    • Document your design decisions
    • Explore alternative approaches
    • Generate starting points for methodology discussions
tip

When facing design choices, ask the LLM for input. This can provide documentation for your choices and serve as a starting point for further refinement.

API Example:​

# assisted by Cursor AI
def analyze_data(data):
    llm_response = client.chat.completions.create(
        model="gpt-4",
        messages=[
            {"role": "system", "content": "You analyze economic data."},
            {"role": "user", "content": f"Analyze: {data}"}
        ],
        temperature=0.2
    )
    return llm_response.choices[0].message.content

Essential LLM Use-Cases for Future Researchers​

  1. Cheap second opinions: Get quick feedback on ideas or analyses.
  2. Devil's advocate: Challenge your assumptions and identify potential weaknesses.
  3. Workshop survey questions and experimental stimuli: Refine research instruments.
  4. Taking over tedious tasks: Automate repetitive, text-based, or time-consuming processes.
  5. Improving project documentation: Generate and maintain comprehensive documentation.
caution

While LLMs can significantly enhance research productivity, always maintain critical thinking and rigorous verification processes.
