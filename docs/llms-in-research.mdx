## Benefits of LLMs in Research

A comprehensive survey published in *Nature* revealed how researchers are currently benefiting from generative AI technologies. The survey highlights that LLMs are particularly valuable for researchers whose first language isn't English, with 56% of respondents citing this as a major benefit. Additionally, many researchers find that LLMs make coding faster (45%) and help summarize existing research (40%).

## Privacy Considerations When Using LLMs

When incorporating LLMs into your research workflow, privacy should be a primary concern. Commercial LLMs are trained on vast datasets, and importantly, they often retain the data you send to them through prompts and file uploads. This data may become part of the model's training data for future versions, creating potential privacy issues.

### Key Privacy Concerns

Before sharing information with an LLM, carefully evaluate the sensitivity of your data. Several types of content should raise immediate red flags:

- Early drafts of unpublished papers that contain novel ideas or findings
- Raw research data, especially if protected by non-disclosure agreements
- Personal information such as names, email addresses, or other identifiable data

Remember that regulatory frameworks like GDPR still apply when using LLMs, particularly when your data might be processed outside the European Union.

## Institutional LLM Solutions

### ERUDITE at Erasmus University

For researchers concerned about data privacy, Erasmus University Rotterdam offers ERUDITE, an institutional LLM solution. This platform provides:

- Access to GPT-4o through Microsoft Teams
- Enhanced data security for sensitive research materials
- Chat-based interface for research assistance

### Open-Source LLMs on Snellius

The Dutch national supercomputer, Snellius, offers another secure option for researchers. By hosting open-source LLMs on Snellius, you can ensure your data remains within the secure environment of the supercomputer without being transmitted to external servers.

### Ollama for Local LLM Deployment

[Ollama](https://ollama.com/) provides a framework for running smaller LLMs directly on your own machine. This free, open-source solution enables:

- Complete privacy as data never leaves your computer
- Integration with other tools and frameworks
- Flexibility to use different open-source models based on your needs

## Institutional Guidelines for LLM Use

Understanding your institution's guidelines for LLM use is essential for responsible research practices. At Erasmus University Rotterdam, the guidelines clearly distinguish between acceptable and prohibited use cases.

### Acceptable Use Cases
LLMs can appropriately support research through:
- Literature reviews for personal understanding
- Assistance with coding tasks
- Refinement of written content
- Support during ideation phases

### Prohibited Use Cases
Researchers should avoid:
- Submitting unmarked LLM-generated writing as their own
- Using LLMs to commit academic fraud
- Presenting the substantive contributions of an LLM as their own work

The fundamental principle is that LLMs should augment, not replace, the research process. They are tools to enhance researcher productivity and creativity, not substitutes for scholarly expertise.

## Nature's Guidelines for LLM Use

Nature has published comprehensive [guidelines](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-025-01069-0) for using LLMs in research that emphasize transparency and responsible authorship.

### Transparency Requirements
When publishing research that utilized LLMs, Nature requires:
- Clear disclosure of LLM use in the methods section
- Specification of the model version and provider
- Details about the prompt engineering process
- Explanation of verification procedures used to validate LLM outputs
- Inclusion of prompts in supplementary materials

### Authorship Standards
Regarding authorship, Nature stipulates that:
- LLMs cannot be listed as authors on papers
- Human authors must take full responsibility for LLM-generated content
- All LLM-generated content must be fact-checked
- Appropriate citation of LLM providers is necessary
- Documentation should clearly indicate which text was generated by LLMs

## LLMs for Research Ideation

Stremersch et al. (2024) discuss how generating great research ideas remains a critical skill, and generative AI can play a supportive role in this process. One principle of successful ideation is volume—having more ideas increases the likelihood of finding excellent ones.

However, Girotra et al. (2023) caution that ideas from LLMs are a "dime a dozen," suggesting that while LLMs can generate numerous ideas, researchers must still apply critical evaluation and refinement.

## Silicone Sampling: LLMs as Synthetic Respondents

An emerging research application of LLMs is "silicone sampling"—using AI models as synthetic respondents. Studies by Argyle et al. (2023) and Sarstedt et al. (2024) have explored how LLMs can function as stand-ins for human participants in certain research contexts.

Arora et al. (2025) demonstrate that synthetic respondents can yield valuable qualitative insights, though with important limitations. This approach may be particularly useful for preliminary research, hypothesis generation, or when access to specific populations is limited.

## LLMs for Research Design Decisions

When faced with research design choices, LLMs can serve as helpful consultants. They can provide documentation for your methodological decisions and offer starting points for consideration. This doesn't replace researcher judgment but can help explore options and considerations more efficiently.

## API vs. Chat Interfaces: Choosing Your Approach

There are two primary ways to interact with LLMs in research: through chat interfaces or API integration.

### Chat Interfaces
Chat interfaces like ChatGPT or Claude offer:
- Interactive, conversational experiences
- No programming requirements
- Excellent tools for exploration and brainstorming
- Limited customization options

A typical chat interaction might look like:
```
User: Analyze this data trend
LLM: I notice three key patterns...
User: What might explain the outliers?
LLM: The outliers could be due to...
```

### API Integration
For more advanced applications, API integration provides:
- Programmatic access to LLM capabilities
- Custom application development
- System-level integration possibilities
- Greater control and flexibility
- The ability to incorporate multiple LLMs in one workflow

```python
# assisted by Cursor AI
def analyze_data(data):
    llm_response = client.chat.completions.create(
        model="gpt-4",
        messages=[
            {"role": "system", "content": "You analyze economic data."},
            {"role": "user", "content": f"Analyze: {data}"}
        ],
        temperature=0.2
    )
    return llm_response.choices[0].message.content
```

## Building Your Academic LLM Toolbox

LLMs can serve multiple valuable functions in your research workflow:
- Providing inexpensive second opinions on your work
- Playing devil's advocate to challenge your assumptions
- Helping workshop survey questions and experimental stimuli
- Taking over tedious, repetitive text-based tasks
- Improving project documentation and organization

By thoughtfully incorporating these tools while adhering to ethical guidelines and transparency principles, researchers can enhance their productivity while maintaining the integrity of their scholarly work.
