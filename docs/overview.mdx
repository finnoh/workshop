# Workshop Overview

Welcome to the Programming Essentials Workshop series! Choose a workshop track that matches your interests and learning goals.

## Available Workshops

### 🔄 Workflow Management

Master the essential tools and practices for modern research workflows.

- **Cloud Computing**: Learn to leverage cloud resources for your research
- **Results Curation**: Best practices for organizing and presenting research results
- **GenAI and Programming**: Integrate AI tools into your development workflow

[Get Started with Workflow Management →](/docs/workflow_management/cloud-computing)

### 🤖 LLMs in Research

Explore how Large Language Models can enhance your research process.

- **Core Concepts**: Understanding the fundamentals of LLMs
- **LLMs in Research**: Practical applications in academic contexts
- **Environmental Considerations**: Understanding and managing AI's environmental impact

[Explore LLMs in Research →](/docs/llms_research/llm-core-concepts)

## Getting Started

1. Choose a workshop track that interests you
2. Start with the first module in that track
3. Follow the exercises and examples
4. Join the discussion and share your insights

Each workshop is designed to be self-paced and includes practical exercises to reinforce your learning.
